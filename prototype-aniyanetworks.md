## 2. Competitor research (to do: Meta Ad Library)

I could not pull ads from the Ad Library in this session, and a web search returned nothing useful. Do this part by hand at facebook.com/ads/library (country: Canada, then US; ad type: all).

**Search keywords:** GoHighLevel automation, GoHighLevel agency, AI receptionist, AI voice agent, missed call text back, HVAC marketing automation, home service CRM, n8n automation agency, workflow automation small business.

**For each of 5 competitors, record:**
- Advertiser name and how long the ad has run (a long run suggests a winner)
- Format (static, video, carousel, UGC)
- Hook (first line or first 3 seconds)
- Angle (time saved, missed leads, cost cut, done-for-you, AI novelty)
- Offer (free audit, demo, trial, price) and CTA

**Then summarize:** the top 3 angles competitors lean on, the offers they use, and the angle nobody is using.

## 3. Angles

1. **Time back:** manual admin costs hours every week (40% cut).
2. **Missed leads:** a missed call or slow reply loses a job. An AI receptionist and instant SMS follow-up fix this.
3. **Done-for-you:** no learning a new tool; they build it and fix it.
4. **Engineer credibility:** built by a DevOps engineer, not a template reseller.
5. **Risk-free try:** a free live AI demo.
